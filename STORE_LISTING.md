### Description

Wren Pocket Studio is the phone companion for turning an idea into a product.

Describe a website, a web app, or a native app. You can type it, say it, or attach a picture, a document, a site address, or a zip. Wren asks what is missing, names the audience, and writes a brief you can save.

The design studio drafts each screen with a purpose, components, and requirements. Approve a screen, reject it, regenerate it, or ask for a change. An approved screen stays as it is until you change it.

When the design is approved, you can open a development job for a remote coding agent. The job keeps the approved screens attached. You can add instructions later without starting over. The app does not mark a build or a deployment successful until that result has been verified.

Also included:

- A home dashboard with projects, approvals, and activity
- Project creation for a new build, a rebuild, design-to-code, or a feature
- Share links and embed code you can copy
- An asset library for images, logos, and 3D requests stored on the device
- Profile, password, notification, and privacy controls

Wren is the astronaut in the lime suit. She is the guide through the work, not a separate assistant.
